💡Idiot's guide to the company — What Cohort plc actually does

What it is

A holding company that owns seven small defence-technology firms and lets each run itself. It sells kit and services to navies, armies and governments: sonar for submarines, military radios and satellite terminals, cameras that track drones, electronic-warfare support and training. Listed on AIM since 2006, year-end 30 April.

Where it's based

Reading, England. Businesses in the UK (SEA, Chess, MASS, MCL), Germany (ELAC sonar), Portugal (EID radios), Australia (EM Solutions satellite terminals). About 1,700 staff.

How it makes money

Two halves. Communications & Intelligence (roughly half of sales, most of the profit): radios, satellite dishes, data and electronic-warfare work — shorter contracts, ~20% profit margins. Sensors & Effectors (the other half of sales, a fifth of the profit): sonar, gun/drone trackers, naval launchers — long multi-year builds, margins stuck around 7–9% because big development projects (the Italian submarine sonar) are booked cautiously until the risk is out.

Who controls the price. Governments and prime contractors (UK MOD, Portuguese Navy, Saab, the Royal Australian Navy) — fixed-price contracts won by tender, so profit depends on delivering what was promised at the cost that was promised.

What's changed recently

Bought EM Solutions (Australia) in Jan-25 for £75m; sold SEA's small Transport arm in Jun-25; renewed and tripled its bank facility to £175m in Apr-26; new Group Chief Operating Officer appointed to fix Chess. Sensors & Effectors' profit and order book both slipped in FY26 while the other half surged.

The bet, in one line: the strong half keeps growing while the weak half's order book stops shrinking and its margin recovers — if only the first happens, the Group beat is a mask.

🍺IDIOT'S GUIDE TO THE ADJUSTED OPERATING PROFIT BRIDGE — why profit went up (£27.5m → £36.3m)

  
📡 The dish that ate the year
+£11.3m
Communications & Intelligence went from £21.1m to £32.4m. Most of it is EM Solutions — the Australian satellite-terminal maker bought in Jan-25 — being inside for twelve months instead of three, plus MASS breaking £50m of sales at a 20% margin. This one row is bigger than the whole rise.

  
🐟 Sonar sold at cost price
−£2.2m
Sensors & Effectors slipped from £12.7m to £10.5m on flat sales. ELAC is still booking the Italian submarine sonar at a "we'll take the profit when it's proven" margin, SEA sold its higher-margin Transport bit, and Chess made a small profit but not the one it was asked for.

  
🏢 Head office, a whisker dearer
−£0.3m
Central costs edged up from £6.3m to £6.6m (the new Group Chief Operating Officer arrives in this line). Rounding-sized.

  
So in one sentence: a satellite-dish business bought last year did nearly all the lifting, and the sonar half handed a slice of it back. Profit falls when: the C&I surge normalises (MCL already has), or the Italian sonar stays booked at cost for another year while Chess misses again; it goes back up when: the first Italian boat is delivered by end-2026 and ELAC starts booking real margin, and Chess's H2 turnaround shows up.
