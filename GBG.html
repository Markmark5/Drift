💡Idiot's guide to the company — What GB Group actually does

What it is

GB Group (GBG) sells software that answers two questions for businesses online: is this person who they say they are, and is this address real? Banks, betting sites, retailers and delivery firms plug it into their sign-up and checkout pages.

Where it's based

Chester, England, with large teams in the US (Atlanta), Australia and Asia. It listed on AIM and moved to the London Stock Exchange Main Market in 2025.

How it makes money

Location (about 30% of sales): address look-up and checking software, sold mostly as yearly subscriptions and through partners. Identity (about 65%): checking a person against credit files, ID documents and other records, paid per check or as a bundle of checks bought in advance; GBG Go is its new self-serve version. Global Fraud Solutions (about 5%): fraud-spotting software licences for banks, mostly in Asia, now under strategic review.

Who controls the price. Big customers do. They negotiate volume deals and can switch provider, so a few large accounts walking away (as in America this year) hits sales quickly. Smaller customers on GBG Go pay list prices.

What's changed recently

New segments from FY26 (Investigate moved into Identity; Fraud renamed GFS). DataTools bought in October 2025. In August 2026 a few large American Identity customers cut back, the FY27 sales guide fell to 1–3% and a goodwill write-down of the American business is coming. The Americas sales chief left.

The bet, in one line: Location and Identity outside America keep growing high-single to double digits, and the question is whether America stops shrinking before it drags the whole group flat.

Stable facts, as at 20 Oct 2026.
