💡Idiot's guide to the company — What Venture Life Group actually does

What it is

A company that owns shelf brands you'd find in Boots, Superdrug, Tesco or on Amazon: Balance Activ (thrush and BV), Lift and Glucogel (sugar boosts for diabetics), Earol (ear-wax spray), Health & Her/Him (menopause and hormone supplements) and, since June 2026, FemiClear (US women's intimate health).

Where it's based

Bracknell, UK. A new US team in Austin, Texas came with FemiClear. The Italian and Swedish factories were sold in 2025 — they now make VLG's products under a ten-year contract.

How it makes money

Two streams. UK/US retail: VLG sells direct to the big chains and Amazon and spends heavily on adverts to pull shoppers in; VLG sets the price, the retailer takes its cut. International partners: distributors in Ireland, Scandinavia, Europe buy in bulk and sell on; order timing is lumpy and the partner controls the pace.

Who controls the price. VLG for its own brands, within what Amazon and the multiples will tolerate; promotional funding is the real lever.

What's changed recently

Sold the factories and the oral-care brands (2025), banked ~£56m, repaid all debt, bought back shares, moved year-end to 31 May, then spent £17.5m up front on FemiClear (June 2026). It is now half the size of the old group and all brand.

The bet, in one line: the ad money buys lasting brand share and the next acquisition soaks up the oversized head office — if not, margins stay stuck in the teens.
