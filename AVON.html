💡Idiot's guide to the company — What Avon Technologies actually does

What it is

Avon makes two things that soldiers and police strap to their heads. Avon Protection makes gas masks, the filters that screw into them, and the suits, boots and gloves that go with them. Team Wendy makes helmets, some built to stop bullets and some built to stop bumps.

Where it's based

Head office is in Melksham, Wiltshire. Masks and filters are made in the UK and at Cadillac in the US. Helmets come out of a new factory in Cleveland, Ohio, and a second site at Salem, after the California site at Irvine was shut. The accounts are in US dollars and the year ends on 30 September.

How it makes money

Masks. Armies buy a mask once and then keep buying filters and spares for decades. Sixteen NATO countries now order through one shared NATO contract, and US police forces buy direct.

Helmets. The US military orders two helmet designs (called NG IHPS and ACH Gen II) in big lumps, with a set number due each month. US police and the Australian army buy the rest.

Who controls the price. Governments do. Orders arrive when budgets and test labs allow, which is why a US government shutdown can stall a quarter without a single customer changing their mind.

What's changed recently

Three years of rebuilding every factory finish this year, and the special charges for it stop after FY26. On 7 October 2026 the company set new aims: sales growing more than 5% a year, 16–18 cents of profit kept from each dollar of sales, and sales above $600m in five years, partly by buying other protection businesses.

The bet, in one line: the mask business keeps selling filters to armies that are re-arming, and the helmet factory finally earns a proper profit now it runs to plan.

Stable facts, as at 7 Oct 2026.
