💡 Idiot's guide to the company — What Genuit Group plc actually does
What it is Genuit Group is the UK's largest maker of pipes, drains, ducting and climate systems for buildings — the stuff hidden in walls and under roads. Based in Leeds, listed on the London Stock Exchange (GEN). About £600m revenue, 3,274 employees. How it makes money Two divisions since January 2026: Water (c.70% of revenue) — plastic pipes and drainage for housebuilders, commercial developers and water utilities; and Climate (c.30%) — ventilation units (Nuaire), underfloor heating (Nu-Heat, Omnie) and magnetic heating filters (Adey). Customers are UK builders, installers and water companies. Polypipe is its best-known brand. Who controls what CEO Joe Vorih (joined 2023) and CFO Tim Pullen run day-to-day. Strategy is "Sustainable Solutions for Growth" — pushing sales of products with an environmental story (recycled polymer, rainwater attenuation, ventilation mandated by building regs). The Genuit Business System (GBS) is an internal lean programme that management cites as the main driver of margin progression. Recent structural events Seven acquisitions since 2021 (Adey, Nu-Heat, Plura; Sky Garden, Omnie in 2024; Monodraught, Davidson Holdings in H2 2025). Segment structure changed twice — three business units (SBS/WMS/CMS) from 2023, collapsed into two divisions (Water/Climate) from 2026. Each acquisition inflates reported revenue growth and makes the like-for-like figure the only honest read on demand.

A business that fixes pipes and moves air — steadily profitable through the UK construction cycle, with a self-help margin story that has so far held through two years of falling volumes; the open question is whether the same playbook works against simultaneous volume weakness and cost inflation.

Turn methodology & restatement basis Comparison basis, hard-coded per metric type
  • Rate metrics (Ranks 1, 3, 4, 5) — the value is the rate. Scored on the change in that rate versus the seasonally matched prior period: H1 vs H1, H2 vs H2, FY vs FY. Never H1 vs H2. Line 2 carries the emoji and the pp movement.
  • Flow metrics (Rank 2) — value plus YoY% on line 1, comparing like period to like period. The scored element on line 2 is the change in that YoY growth rate versus the prior seasonally matched period — i.e. acceleration, not growth.
  • Snapshot metrics — none in this build. GEN publishes no order book, pipeline or backlog figure on a consistent basis, so no metric carries a sequential position-to-position comparison. Leverage was considered and dropped to the balance-sheet sweep, where the Tier-1 trigger already covers it.
  • Display basis — half-grain flow rows display seasonally matched YoY with the banded rate-delta as the scored element. Raw half-on-half movement is computed but never displayed in cells; it is seasonal at GEN (H2 margin is structurally above H1 in every year of the record) and would read as a turn when it is only the calendar.
  • Band — ±1.0pp at half grain, adjusted down from the framework default of ±2.0pp on sector amplitude. GEN's margin moves in a 20–140bps range; at ±2.0pp the margin rank could never fire, including the FY25 decline.
