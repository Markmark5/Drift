Turn methodology & restatement basis

Turn methodology (v4.0 display basis). Every YoY, Δrate and arrow is computed in one script, comparison basis hard-coded per metric type: flow (revenue, PBT) displays seasonally matched YoY (H1-vs-H1, H2†-vs-H2†); the scored element is the banded Δrate — the change in that YoY vs the prior seasonally matched period, ±2.0pp H-grain band → 🟢↗ / 🟡→ / 🔴↘. Raw HonH is computed in script and retained here, never displayed: H1'26 vs H2'25† = −1.1%; H2'25† vs H1'25 = −1.8%. Rate (margin) = seasonally matched Δpp against the same band. Snapshot (order book, net cash) = position-to-position; cells carry sequential Δ per the B.4 template; ˢ arrows score the seasonally matched comparison (H1-vs-H1 / FY-vs-FY) because CHH cash phases seasonally — H1 dips every year (sequential and seasonal signs disagree at H1'26: seq −£2.0m, YoY +57%; both shown). YoY for snapshots renders only on the aggregate row. Aggregate rows are transcribed company prints, never summed, never scored — the parent never governs. Derived-only arrows (†) are watch flags (wf) until a reported sub-period corroborates.

Arrow semantics changed vs the v3.10 build: flow arrows now mark rate-of-change (deceleration of decline scores 🟢↗ even at a negative level — the level's sign lives in the red YoY brackets). The verdict card and §2.0 bar were reworded 31 Jul 2026 to the v4.0 basis (hard condition rests on margin compression + Text); full reconciliation of all sections lands at the Sep'26 H1 Results rebuild.
