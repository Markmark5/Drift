💡Idiot's guide to the company — What Microlise Group plc actually does

What it is

Microlise sells the brains and dashboards for lorry fleets. A box in each cab tracks the truck — where it is, how it's driven, when it'll arrive — and the software turns that into fewer wasted miles, safer drivers and less paperwork. Started in 1982, run from Nottingham with offices in France, Australia and India, about 730 staff and over 2,500 clients. Listed on AIM under the ticker SAAS.

How the money comes in

Two taps. Direct: fleet operators pay a yearly subscription per vehicle — sticky, repeat money, £47.1m a year and growing 12%. The customer relationship is Microlise's own. Through truck-makers (OEMs): manufacturers pre-fit Microlise kit in new lorries and pay for it — £13.7m a year and shrinking as renewals lapse, and the truck-makers' build schedules control the pace, not Microlise. One-off hardware and installation fees sit on top of both.

Big recent changes

Bought three software firms in early 2024 (including Enterprise Software Systems). Hit by a cyber attack in October 2024. Cut costs hard in 2025 — £5m a year saved — after profits fell, changed house broker to Canaccord, and swapped its technology chief in January 2026.

The bet in one line: the sticky direct subscriptions outgrow the fading truck-maker side before the market loses patience. — as at 03-Aug-26
