💡Idiot's guide to the company — What Elixirr International plc actually does

The firm

Elixirr is a London-born consulting firm, founded in 2009, that sells advice — strategy, digital builds, data and AI work — to big companies. It calls itself the "challenger consultancy": small senior teams pitching against the giant firms and winning on speed and the price of a partner's actual attention. Listed on AIM in 2020, moved up to the London Stock Exchange's Main Market in July 2025. Nearly two-thirds of the fees now come from America (63% of FY25 revenue).

How the money comes in

Clients pay project fees. Partners — the senior sellers — own chunks of the company, so the person pitching you also profits when the bill gets paid. Growth comes two ways: partners selling more to old and new clients (up 15.3% in FY25 before counting anything bought), and buying small boutique firms outright — nine so far, from digital agencies to a Chicago strategy shop (TRC, Sept 2025, the biggest yet) and a Copenhagen one (Kvadrant, Jan 2026).

Who runs the show

Founder Stephen Newton is still CEO. Co-founder Graham Busby stepped up to Deputy CEO in late 2024 and handed the finance job to Nick Willott.

What's changed lately

The TRC deal was paid for with bank borrowing — so a company that always sat on spare cash ended FY25 owing £24.1m, with £51m of its credit line still unused.

The bet: a firm of owner-salespeople can keep growing 30%+ a year, and the borrowing is a step-stool, not a treadmill.

Turn methodology & restatement basis

One script, bases hard-coded per metric type. Snapshot (net cash/debt): position-to-position at its own disclosure grain, ±£2.0m dead-band on Δ. Flow (revenue, adjusted PBT): like-period only (H1-vs-H1, FY-vs-FY — never H1-vs-9M); Δ shown is the YoY growth; the turn is the change in that growth vs the prior same-type print, ±2.0pp band — direction and acceleration, never absolute level. Rate (organic %, EBITDA margin): seasonally-matched Δ, ±2.0pp band: ≥+2.0pp → 🟢↗, inside band → 🟡→, ≤−2.0pp → 🔴↘. No Q1/Q3 disclosure exists, so no sequential QonQ trigger applies; derived H2 halves are single-route (FY−H1, no corroborating quarter route) and remain watch flags.
