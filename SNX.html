💡Idiot's guide to the company — What Synectics plc actually does
What it is

Synectics builds the camera-and-software kit that lets big organisations watch what's happening across lots of sites at once from one screen — think a casino floor, a police control room, or a fleet of buses — and its own software (Synergy) stitches all those camera feeds and sensors into one live picture instead of a wall of separate boxes. It's a UK company, AIM-listed, run out of Sheffield.

How it makes money

Two shops under one roof. Synectic Systems is the bigger, lumpier one — it sells big integrated surveillance jobs to gaming resorts, energy sites and other high-security operators, so its revenue swings hard whenever one large project lands or finishes (a single South-East Asian casino contract carried a chunk of last year's growth). Ocular Integration (rebranded from the old "security" arm in late 2024) is steadier — it fits out buses, coaches and infrastructure operators like National Grid and West Midlands Police with camera and monitoring systems, so it grows in smaller, more regular steps.

Who's in charge

Amanda Larnder is CEO, now into her second year and mid-way through a self-funded rebuild of how the company sells and delivers its work. Paul Williams joined as CFO in August 2025 and delivered his first set of results in March 2026.

Recent structural event

The former "security" division was rebranded to Ocular Integration in October 2024. FY26 is being run as a deliberate one-year investment pause — cash is being spent on building repeatable, partner-led sales rather than chasing one-off mega-projects.

The one-line argument: a business that just had its best-ever year on the back of one giant contract is now spending its own cash to prove the next good year can come from many smaller ones instead.

Turn methodology & restatement basis

Turn methodology. Every Δ, HonH, rate delta and Turn arrow in this grid is computed in one script, with the comparison basis hard-coded per metric type; the table transcribes script output. Snapshot metrics (order book, net cash) compare position-to-position at their own disclosure grain. Flow metrics (group revenue, adjusted EBITDA, energy revenue) compare like-period-to-like-period only — H1-vs-H1, FY-vs-FY, never H1-vs-9M. Sequential trigger (primary): at this H-grain, any negative HonH fires 🔴↘ on level regardless of year-comp; comp-flags annotate, never suppress. Rate trigger (secondary, banded): Δ(YoY rate) vs prior seasonally-matched period, H-grain ±2.0pp → 🟢↗ / 🟡→ / 🔴↘. Leading metrics are scored on direction of the company-reported delta vs the prior comparable print, never absolute level. First period in a series → —. Derived-only Turns (†) are watch flags until a reported sub-period corroborates; parent tie-out is arithmetic, not corroboration. Parent-vs-latest-sub-period divergence (v3.16): where a parent 🟢/🟡 contains a latest sub-period firing 🔴, the sub-period governs and the parent is flagged front/backward-loaded. Aggregate row (v3.16): parent years never occupy columns — they render on the aggregate row beneath each rank, transcribed from company-printed totals, never summed from the cells above, never scored, no arrows; the FY-grain Δrate values shown there (e.g. FY24 −11.9pp) are historical context only, their scored record retained in the chat build.
